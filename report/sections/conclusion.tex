\section{Conclusion}

This evaluation study emphasize the importance of considering 
not only the best predictive performance but also what is 
the most beneficial in terms of cost when selecting 
which method to use. 
Our findings suggest that while supervised GNNs are 
well suited for high accuracy applications, 
spectral graph embeddings offer a great alternative 
when the computational resources are missing or 
when the robustness to noise is critical.
