\section{Conclusion}

This evaluation study emphasize the importance of considering 
not only the best predictive performance but also what is 
the most beneficial in terms of cost when selecting 
which method to use.
Our findings suggest that GIN consistently achieves the highest classification accuracy, 
both before and after graph perturbations, confirming the strength of supervised GNNs for discriminative tasks.
However, GIN also experiences the largest performance degradation under perturbations, indicating sensitivity to structural noise,
and not forget to menntion its high computational cost.
In contrast, NetLSD exhibits strong clustering behavior and maintains relatively stable accuracy with consistently high AUC scores.
Graph2Vec proves to be the most stable method under perturbations, 
making it particularly suitable for stability analysis, 
although its classification accuracy remains comparatively low.

Overall, while GIN is preferable for high-accuracy 
classification, NetLSD and Graph2Vec offer robust 
and computationally efficient alternatives for 
noise-prone or resource-constrained settings, 
emphasizing the need for holistic evaluation beyond accuracy alone.
